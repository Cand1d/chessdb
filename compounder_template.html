How this is built

The forward path is a bootstrap simulation, not a forecast: the deviation from trend is carried forward as an AR(1) process and the weekly shocks are drawn, with replacement, from the ones the asset actually printed inside the fit window — so the fat tails and the skew survive. Every headline number is the median of the simulated paths; the range beneath it is the 25th to 75th percentile. Hit New draw to resample.

Rules, exactly: an add fires the first week the signal closes at or below your buy threshold, and re-arms only after the signal climbs back 0.5σ above it — so one dip buys once, not every week it lingers. Sells work the same way in reverse. Adds are funded from the sell pile first and only then out of your pocket, which is why out of pocket can sit below total invested. Nothing is rebalanced, and no fees, spreads, slippage or taxes are modelled.

Backtests and simulations describe a rule, not a future. Data from Kraken and Yahoo Finance; page rebuilt __BUILT_AT__. Not investment advice.
